The Mission

Hello! I am AR, also known as arlabs07 online. ARhub was born out of a simple necessity: the need for a unified, clutter-free ecosystem where developers can find powerful tools without the bloat.

What started as a simple collection of scripts has evolved into a comprehensive suite including PurplePDF for document management and the ardev library for rapid frontend development. My goal is to bridge the gap between complex engineering and accessible, beautiful user interfaces.

Technology Stack

The ARhub ecosystem is built on the bleeding edge of web technologies. We believe in the power of the open web.

  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3 (Glassmorphism/Neumorphism), Vanilla JS (ES6+)
  • Performance: Web Assembly (Wasm) for PDF processing
  • Infrastructure: Cloud-native architecture for high availability
  • AI Integration: Custom models powering the Arai assistant
